Rental service policies

Best Rents is a full-service company: we deliver, set up and retrieve every order. Here's what to know before you book.

Order minimums

Minimums do not include sales tax, labor or delivery fees.

SituationMinimum rental
Regular business hours$150
After-hours delivery or retrieval
Before 7:00 AM or after 7:00 PM on weekdays, and all day on weekends		$250

  • Services outside the local coverage area are available to corporate customers only.
  • Apple MacBook rentals are available to corporate customers only.
  • Laptops for personal use (weddings, quinceañeras, bat mitzvahs and similar celebrations) must be rented together with other AV equipment.

Delivery service

Customer pickup of rental equipment is not available. All AV orders are delivered, set up and retrieved by Best Rents.

  • For same-day or next-day needs, please call 305-891-2399.
  • Freight/delivery charges cover delivery and pickup at your office or venue. Basic setup may be included.
  • Orders outside South Florida may ship via FedEx®, UPS® or other carriers.
  • Large orders or heavy items may incur additional service or labor charges.
  • Repeat deliveries or pickups may cost extra. Contact us in advance to reschedule.
  • Someone must be available to sign for equipment or services received.
  • Trade shows and exhibit halls may charge drayage or union fees, which are paid by the exhibitor.
